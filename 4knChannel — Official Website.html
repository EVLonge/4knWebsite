About

4knChannel

4knChannel is the music project of Trippy — a producer and artist based in Nigeria, building a sound that refuses genre assignment.

The catalogue sits somewhere between electronic trance and jazz harmony, lo-fi texture and live rock energy. Every track is structural — built with intention around energy arcs, not just vibes.

This is a bedroom project made for the world. No label. No formula. Just the work.

Drawn from the worlds of Coldplay, Migos, André 3000, Dr. Dre, Queen, Logic, Sad Night Dynamite and Twenty One Pilots.
